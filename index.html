An Execution Layer for AI in Drug Discovery

NuoliHealth provides routing tools and intelligence to bots, agents, and drug developers to turn AI-generated biotech ideas into executable preclinical work: assay selection, CRO/CDMO provider matching, experiment scoping, and RFQ-ready work packets.
