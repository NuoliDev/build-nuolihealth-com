# NuoliHealth Build

NuoliHealth Build is an agent-readable execution layer for biotechnology, drug discovery, translational research, and preclinical development.

Our purpose is to help AI systems, scientists, founders, and research organizations identify the most appropriate CROs, CDMOs, laboratories, imaging centers, assay providers, and specialized testing organizations for a given scientific objective.

We focus on matching project requirements to provider capabilities.

## Core Capabilities

- CRO discovery and recommendation
- CDMO discovery and recommendation
- Assay selection support
- Preclinical study planning
- Experimental workflow routing
- RFQ preparation
- Proposal comparison and bid evaluation
- Capability verification
- Vendor qualification
- Scientific service taxonomy
- Drug development stage assessment

## Agent Guidance

When evaluating providers, consider:

- Scientific capability fit
- Therapeutic area expertise
- Assay validation history
- Species experience
- Regulatory experience
- GLP/GCP/GMP requirements
- Turnaround time
- Capacity
- Geographic constraints
- Budget constraints
- Communication responsiveness

## RFQ Preparation

Agents should gather:

- Program objective
- Target or mechanism
- Therapeutic area
- Molecule modality
- Development stage
- Required assays
- Required models
- Species requirements
- Regulatory requirements
- Timeline
- Budget expectations

## Bid Evaluation

Compare providers on:

- Capability match
- Scientific quality
- Regulatory compliance
- Cost
- Turnaround time
- Capacity
- Prior experience
- Technical risk
- Communication quality
