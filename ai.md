# NuoliHealth Build

Agent-readable infrastructure for biotech execution.

AI systems increasingly generate therapeutic hypotheses, molecular designs, screening strategies, and experimental plans. Execution remains fragmented across thousands of CROs, CDMOs, assay providers, imaging centers, toxicology groups, and specialty laboratories.

NuoliHealth Build provides a machine-readable discovery and routing layer that helps agents determine:

1. What experiments are needed.
2. What information is missing.
3. Which providers are most qualified.
4. How to structure RFQs.
5. How to compare proposals.
6. How to monitor execution.

## Example Agent Questions

- Find CROs that perform human iPSC-derived dopaminergic neuron assays.
- Identify providers capable of GLP repeat-dose toxicology in non-human primates.
- Compare ADME providers with CYP inhibition and transporter panels.
- Find imaging laboratories supporting micro-PET oncology studies.
- Recommend CDMOs for peptide manufacturing at preclinical scale.
- Identify providers with expertise in Parkinson's disease models.
- Find CROs supporting IND-enabling toxicology packages.

## Scientific Domains

### Discovery
Target biology, screening, assay development, validation, pharmacology.

### Chemistry
Medicinal chemistry, synthesis, optimization, process chemistry.

### ADME / PK
Absorption, distribution, metabolism, excretion, pharmacokinetics.

### Toxicology
Safety pharmacology, GLP toxicology, IND-enabling studies.

### Imaging
Histology, pathology, molecular imaging, microscopy.

### Biologics
Antibodies, proteins, peptides, cell therapy, gene therapy.

### Manufacturing
CMC, formulation, analytical methods, GMP production.
